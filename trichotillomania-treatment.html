Trichotillomania treatments compared

What actually works for hair pulling? Habit reversal training, the ComB model and acceptance-based therapy compared, with the research behind each and where an app fits.

Updated 14 October 2026 · By the Trich Reset team · Every claim links to its source below

The short answer

The treatment with the strongest evidence for trichotillomania is behaviour therapy built around habit reversal training (HRT). A 2020 meta-analysis of 24 randomised trials (857 people) found a large benefit for HRT-based therapy.[1] The skills it teaches (noticing the urge, keeping your hands busy, changing your setup and riding the urge out) are things you can start practising today.

What habit reversal training involves

  1. Awareness training. Learning exactly when, where and how you pull, including the warning signs before your hand moves.
  2. Competing response. Doing something with your hands for about a minute that makes pulling impossible: making a fist, holding an object, sitting on your hands.
  3. Stimulus control. Changing the setup: gloves while reading, a hat at the desk, dimmer bathroom lights.
  4. Social support. Someone who gently notices and encourages you.

Common questions

What is the most effective treatment for trichotillomania?

Behaviour therapy built around habit reversal training (HRT). A 2020 meta-analysis of 24 randomised trials found a large benefit for HRT-based therapy compared with control conditions (Farhat et al., Depression and Anxiety, 2020).

Is there medication for trichotillomania?

Some medicines and supplements have been studied, with mixed results, and none is approved specifically for trichotillomania. A GP or psychiatrist can talk through whether anything is right for you. Behaviour therapy has the strongest evidence.

Can an app help with trichotillomania?

An app is not a replacement for therapy, but it can help you practise the same skills between sessions or while you wait for one: noticing urges, riding them out and tracking your triggers. Trich Reset adds guided audio for the moment the urge arrives.

How do I find a trichotillomania therapist?

The TLC Foundation for Body-Focused Repetitive Behaviors keeps a directory of clinicians trained in BFRBs at bfrb.org. Ask any therapist whether they use habit reversal training or the ComB model.
